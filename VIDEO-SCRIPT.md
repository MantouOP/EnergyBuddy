# EnergyBuddy — 4 minute 30 second video script

This script is deliberately under five minutes. Rehearse once with a timer; aim for a calm pace rather than filling every second.

## 0:00–0:25 — Hook

**Visual:** Start on the Today screen.

“A student can have two free hours on Thursday and still have no energy left to use them. Calendars show us where time goes, but they do not show the mental, physical and social cost of everything we carry. That is why burnout often feels sudden, even though the load has been building for days.”

## 0:25–0:55 — Problem and user

**Visual:** Point to today's commitments and three batteries.

“Our target user is a university student balancing classes, assignments, a part-time job, errands and social responsibilities. Their plans are fragmented, their capacity is invisible, and most wellbeing tools react after stress is already high. We wanted to give that student an early warning and something practical to do with it.”

## 0:55–1:20 — Solution

**Visual:** Show the EnergyBuddy name and weather card.

“This is EnergyBuddy: a workload companion that turns personal energy into a seven-day weather forecast. It combines a quick check-in with the expected energy cost of upcoming commitments. It is not a medical diagnosis. It is an explainable workload-risk estimate designed to support better decisions.”

## 1:20–1:55 — Three batteries

**Visual:** Scroll or point to Mental, Physical and Social.

“Instead of one vague wellness score, EnergyBuddy uses three batteries. Mental energy captures deep work and concentration. Physical energy reflects sleep, commuting and shifts. Social energy reflects meetings, group projects and interaction. The student can correct these estimates with a 15-second check-in, keeping the model personal without creating another journaling chore.”

## 1:55–2:35 — Forecast

**Action:** Open the Forecast tab.

“The forecast makes the coming week glanceable. Clear days have a safe reserve, Cloudy and Heavy days need attention, and a Storm means the projected reserve is below fifteen percent. Thursday is stormy: the student begins with sixty-one percent energy but faces eighty-five percent demand. EnergyBuddy explains why—two deep-work blocks and a late shift with no meal or reset between them.”

## 2:35–3:35 — Standout demo

**Action:** Open What-if and show both plans.

“Awareness alone is not enough, so our defining feature is What-If Mode. EnergyBuddy keeps the database lab and paid shift because they are essential. It splits the assignment into a focused seventy-five-minute block, protects lunch as recovery, and moves flexible revision to a clearer day. The student can inspect every change before accepting it.”

**Action:** Click Apply balanced plan, then return to Forecast.

“After applying the plan, Thursday changes from Storm to Cloudy. The ending reserve rises from seven to thirty-one percent—a twenty-four percentage-point improvement without removing an essential commitment. This before-and-after change is the core value of EnergyBuddy.”

## 3:35–4:05 — Novelty and feasibility

**Visual:** Keep the updated forecast visible.

“Todo lists organise tasks, and self-care apps encourage recovery. EnergyBuddy connects the two through three-dimensional energy, familiar weather language and an actionable forecast. The prototype uses a transparent weighted model, so every warning can be explained. Our build-phase scope is focused: check-ins, energy-aware tasks, forecasting, What-If Mode and secure personal storage.”

## 4:05–4:30 — Impact and close

**Visual:** Return to Today or show the final balanced plan.

“EnergyBuddy helps students notice cumulative load earlier, understand what is causing it, and protect recovery without abandoning what matters. Calendars tell students when they are busy. EnergyBuddy helps them see when they are running out—and change course before the storm.”

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p with browser zoom around 100%.
- Close notifications and unrelated tabs.
- Keep the pointer still unless demonstrating an interaction.
- Use the exact order: Today → Forecast → What-if → Apply → Forecast.
- Keep the final cut between 3:00 and 5:00.
- Upload as **Unlisted**, not Private.
- Test the video link while signed out before submission.
